Pulse · · · C & Linux & Monitor

Created 2026
A small monitoring system, written purely in C. Developed in the open, and licensed under MIT.

Currently the main project, having multiple contributors. Its main idea is not to replace the big competitors.

Its simplicity is on purpose. You can install it, and run it instantly, with no configuration whatsoever. Pulse remains one of my biggest projects to day.

zeP · · · Zig & PM

Created 2025
A package manager for the Zig programming language. The development of it was really quick, however the development stopped after a while.

Because Zig (during 2025/26) was in its 0.x.x era, a lot of the language changed a lot.

zeP will still remain one of my biggest projects, and my start to my low-level era.

PDC · · · C & PNG & 3D

Created 2026
Simple PNG decompressor written purely in C. The goal of the project, is to turn a 2D image into a 3D landscape.

However, the development was quickly ceased after the start of my dual-study.

PDC is still a big project, that helped me learn C very quickly, and helped me build my base knowledge.
